📅 Adventuring Day Budget

Tool 16 of 20 · Part of the free 5e HP Forge toolkit · Runs offline, no tracking

The Dungeon Master's Guide's most ignored guideline is also its most important: a character can handle roughly six to eight medium-or-hard encounters per day, and the XP budget for that day is printed per level on page 84. This tab does that budgeting for you. Set the encounter count, party size, average level, short rests and a difficulty mix — balanced, mostly easy, mostly medium, mostly hard or boss-heavy — and the tool distributes the day's budget across the encounters.

The Encounter Timeline card makes the resource drain visible. Add encounters one by one with a difficulty and an HP-drain percentage, insert short rests where your dungeon actually has a safe room, and the timeline shows the party's resources decaying across the day. It is the antidote to the one-big-fight-then-long-rest pattern that lets daily-spell classes dominate while short-rest classes nap through the plot.

Dungeon pacing is a design skill, and this tab is its practice tool. Six medium fights that feel like a gauntlet beat three deadly ones that feel like three separate games — and the budget math proves it before you commit to a map.

What you can do

  • Full adventuring-day XP budgets by party level (DMG p.84)
  • Encounter count, party size and difficulty mix as simple inputs
  • Short rests priced into the day's economy
  • Visual encounter timeline with per-encounter HP drain
  • Boss-heavy mode for climactic-day structures
  • Pairs with the Encounter Builder to make each entry real

How the math works

The daily budget scales steeply with level — a 5th-level party's day is several times a 1st-level party's — and the tool splits that total across your encounters according to the chosen mix. 'Mostly easy' and 'boss-heavy' sum to the same XP; they spread it very differently.

Short rests are entries on the same timeline as fights, so a mid-dungeon break visibly splits the day's drain into two waves instead of one continuous slide toward empty.

Nova problem: if players spend every resource in one fight and rest, add time pressure — the timeline's drain column is the argument for why the ritual finishes in four encounters, not one.

Open this tool

Adventuring Day Budget lives inside the main calculator. Open the app and click the Day Budget tab — the sixteenth one in the tab bar. Everything computes client-side and works offline once the page has loaded.
