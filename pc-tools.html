💀 PC & Party Tools

Tool 13 of 20 · Part of the free 5e HP Forge toolkit · Runs offline, no tracking

Six small, sharp tools for the things players usually track on index cards. The Death Save Tracker keeps three successes and three failures as tappable dots, with buttons for a real roll, an instant natural 20 — consciousness with 1 HP — and a natural 1, which counts two failures at once. The Condition Tracker is a wall of tappable chips covering the core conditions, exhaustion levels and Concentrating, each surfacing the rules text that applies.

The Hit Dice Recovery card runs long-rest math — total hit dice, die size, Constitution modifier, current versus maximum HP — returning how much of the pool a night's sleep actually restores. The Exhaustion Tracker walks levels 0 through 6, from disadvantage on checks to speed 0 to death, with each rung's penalty spelled out. The Spell Slot Calculator generates full grids for full, half and third casters at any level, plus the Warlock's pact magic, where every slot matures at a single slot level.

The Temporary HP Tracker completes the set: stack temp-HP sources, apply damage, and it deducts from the temporary pool before touching real HP — the rule most tables get wrong — clearing everything on a long rest.

What you can do

  • Death saves with a dot UI, crit success and crit fail buttons
  • Condition chips with the actual rules text on tap
  • Long-rest hit-dice recovery with die size and Con mod
  • Exhaustion ladder 0–6 with every tier's penalty
  • Spell-slot grids for full, half and third casters plus pact magic
  • Temp-HP stack with correct damage ordering and long-rest reset

How the math works

The spell-slot grids follow the standard caster tables, including the Warlock's distinctive economy — few slots, all of one level, recovered on a short rest. Temp HP absorbs damage before real HP, and only the best pool counts.

Every control here is a tap or two, because these tools are used mid-combat: big targets, single-purpose buttons and instant feedback, not menus and documentation.

Table trick: keep this tab open on a tablet. The death-save dots and condition chips replace half the crib sheet, and nothing needs an account or a login.
