⚔️ Advanced Combat Calculators

Tool 11 of 20 · Part of the free 5e HP Forge toolkit · Runs offline, no tracking

Six precision calculators share this tab, covering every recurring arithmetic headache at the table. The Damage Per Round calculator models a full attack routine — attacks per round, damage dice, modifier, attack bonus, target AC and crit dice — and reports expected damage with hit chance priced in. The AoE calculator handles spells like fireball: damage dice, save DC, average save bonus, target count and a resistance rate.

The Critical Hit calculator supports expanded crit ranges for champions and their 19–20s. The Save DC and Attack Bonus calculators each offer a from-CR mode that fills in the value a creature of that rating is expected to have — instant calibration for homebrew. The Healing calculator handles cure spells against conscious, downed, dying or dead targets, including the rule details that matter when someone is at 0 HP.

Together they replace the scrap-paper math behind every homebrew monster and every 'can I actually kill it this turn?' pause. The defaults are preloaded with typical values, so you can experiment the moment the tab opens.

What you can do

  • Full DPR with hit chance, target AC and crit dice included
  • AoE damage with save DC, save bonus and target count
  • Critical-hit math supporting 19–20 and 18–20 crit ranges
  • Save DC and attack bonus with auto-fill from CR
  • Healing math for conscious, downed, dying and dead targets
  • Six tools on one tab — no juggling mid-session

How the math works

Hit chance is (21 + attack bonus − AC) ÷ 20, clamped to the 5–95% extremes, and a crit adds the crit dice once more. The save-DC baseline is 8 + proficiency + ability modifier, and the from-CR modes pull the expected values straight from the monster-creation tables.

Every result is printed as plain arithmetic rather than a bare number, so a bad input — an AC typed as 51, a dice string with a typo — is visible before it silently skews the plan.

Homebrew shortcut: use the from-CR modes to pick an expected attack bonus and DC first, then design the monster's attacks to match — offense stays consistent with the rating.

Open this tool
