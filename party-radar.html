📊 Party Composition Radar

Tool 17 of 20 · Part of the free 5e HP Forge toolkit · Runs offline, no tracking

Every party has a gap, and the gap stays invisible until the saving throw that matters. The Party Composition Radar draws six axes of combat capability — single-target DPR, AoE damage, healing capacity, tankiness, utility and control — as a radar chart of the whole party's shape. The profile makes holes obvious at a glance.

Enter the party's aggregate numbers in the six fields, press Generate Radar Chart, and the canvas renders the profile immediately, with an automated analysis underneath calling out the strong and weak axes. Tankiness is entered as HP times AC, the honest durability proxy; utility is counted in skills and control in save-or-suck effects, on simple bounded scales. The defaults show a balanced level-5 party; your table will not be balanced, and that is the point.

The chart is a conversation starter as much as a diagnostic. Show it to your players before the deadly dungeon and let them see the healer gap themselves — it lands better than any DM hint, and it lets the players patch the hole with a potion budget or a hireling instead of you silently nerfing the dungeon.

What you can do

  • Six capability axes: DPR, AoE, healing, tankiness, utility, control
  • Instant canvas radar chart, fully offline
  • Automated gap analysis beneath the chart
  • Tankiness modeled as HP × AC, the honest durability proxy
  • Utility and control on simple bounded scales
  • The pre-dungeon sanity check for party structure

How the math works

The six raw numbers are normalized onto a common scale to draw the polygon. Tankiness multiplies HP by AC because a character with huge HP and poor AC bleeds far faster than either number alone suggests — 80 HP at AC 12 dies quicker than 50 HP at AC 18 in most fights.

Rough estimates are fine — the chart reads trends rather than decimals, so guestimate the party totals from character sheets and the shape will still tell the truth.

Re-run it after every level-up or roster change. Parties drift quietly — a multiclass, a new familiar or one dead hireling moves axes that nobody rechecks until the fight goes wrong.

Open this tool

Party Composition Radar lives inside the main calculator. Open the app and click the Party Radar tab — the seventeenth one in the tab bar. Everything computes client-side and works offline once the page has loaded.
