⚖️ Comparison Mode

Tool 8 of 20 · Part of the free 5e HP Forge toolkit · Runs offline, no tracking

Is 8d10+24 tougher than 15d6-5? The averages are close — and 'close' is where surprises live. Comparison Mode puts up to four hit-dice configurations side by side and grades them on the numbers that matter: minimum, maximum and average hit points, plus a durability percentage relative to the strongest build.

The slots come preloaded with two contrasting builds so the idea is obvious immediately: a steady Medium brute on d10s against a swingy creature on d6s with a bigger flat bonus. Replace them with anything — two candidate builds for the same boss, a monster before and after a template, or the same creature at two levels. Slots three and four are optional; two are often enough to settle an argument.

Combined with the Probability Dashboard, Compare is how you stop designing by folklore. When the table insists two builds are 'basically the same HP', you can answer with a table instead of an opinion — and quietly pick the one whose floor holds up when the dice turn hostile.

What you can do

  • Up to four expressions compared at once, two minimum
  • Side-by-side minimum, maximum and average hit points
  • Durability percentage relative to the strongest configuration
  • Instantly exposes the variance differences averages hide
  • Same expression syntax as the main calculator
  • One pass, no reload — all results appear together

How the math works

Every build is scored with the same NdM±K arithmetic as the main calculator, and the durability column scales each average against the group's strongest. The interesting number is usually the spread: 8d10+24 spans 32 to 104 while 15d6-5 spans 10 to 85 — one never drops below a fightable floor, the other can.

Because all four builds are scored in a single pass with identical arithmetic, the comparison is fair by construction: no rounding differences, no forgotten modifiers, no re-typing. Swap expressions and re-run as often as you like — nothing else on the page changes.

Boss picks: when choosing between candidates, weigh the minimum. A boss whose worst case is still respectable is more reliable than one whose average merely looks good.

Open this tool

Comparison Mode lives inside the main calculator. Open the app and click the Compare tab — the eighth one in the tab bar. Everything computes client-side and works offline once the page has loaded.
