Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025 · Applies to dnd-hp-calculator.pages.dev and every page on this site

Short version: this site collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no cookies set by us, no data sent anywhere. Everything you type is processed in your browser and stored only on your own device. You can erase it all at any time by clearing the site's data.

1. What this site collects

Nothing. The site is a static set of files served from Cloudflare Pages. It has no backend, no database and no form that submits anywhere. The JavaScript never transmits your input to any server, and there are no third-party scripts, fonts, images or requests of any kind.

2. What stays on your device (local storage)

To remember your work between visits, the app uses your browser's built-in localStorage — a storage area that belongs to your browser, is not accessible to other websites, and is never uploaded anywhere. The following keys are used:

KeyWhat it holdsWhen it is written
ddhp_lastSessionAuto-save snapshot of the calculator inputs (expressions, creature fields, PC builder fields, etc.).Automatically, 1.5 s after you change any input.
ddhp_savesYour named save slots (up to 20), created with the 💾 Save button.Only when you click Save.
ddhp_darkYour light/dark theme choice (true / false).When you toggle the theme.
ddhp_roundingYour HP rounding preference from the Settings tab (round vs. floor).When you change it in Settings.
ddhp_xpHistoryXP entries from the XP Tracker tab.When you add XP events.

All of this data stays in that browser, on that device. Clearing the site's data (or using the backup/restore controls in the app's Settings tab) removes it permanently.

3. Cookies

This site does not set any cookies itself. Cloudflare, which hosts the site, may use its own strictly functional cookies (such as __cf_bm) to protect against abuse and route your requests — this is standard for any website served through Cloudflare and is not controlled by this site. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

4. Share links

The 🔗 Share button builds a link that encodes a handful of calculator fields (hit-dice expression, creature numbers, PC builder fields and similar) directly into the URL's #s=… fragment using base64. The link is copied to your clipboard and only leaves your device if you choose to paste and send it to someone. The fragment part of a URL is not normally sent to web servers.

5. Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes standard technical request data (IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp) to deliver the site, defend against attacks and comply with law. This site's owner does not access, use or sell that data for any other purpose. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy for details.

6. No analytics, ads or profiling

There are no analytics services, no advertising networks, no social media embeds, no fingerprinting and no cross-site tracking on this site — ever.

7. Your rights and controls

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted by the site, there is nothing to export, correct or delete on the server side. Everything the app knows about you is on your device, and you control it:

  • Delete everything: use your browser's "Clear site data" / "Clear browsing data" for this site.
  • Delete in-app: Settings tab → backup/restore and the individual clear buttons (Clear Party, Clear Monsters, Reset, etc.).
  • Take your data with you: Settings tab → export a JSON backup, then import it in any other browser or device.

Under regulations such as the GDPR or CCPA you have rights over your personal data; since this site processes none on any server, those rights are satisfied by design — the only data involved is yours, on your machine, under your control.

8. Children

This site is a dice-math tool intended for general use and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from any visitor of any age.

9. Changes to this policy

If the site ever adds a feature that changes this picture (it currently has none planned), this page will be updated before that feature ships, and the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will also be noted on the Changelog page.
