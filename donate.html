♥ Support the 5e HP Forge

Free forever · No ads, no paywalls, no accounts · Your support keeps it that way

The 5e HP Forge is built and maintained by one Dungeon Master, in spare time carved out between sessions, for the love of the game and for every table that needs a monster statblock at midnight. It has no ads, no analytics, no sponsored placements and no locked features — the full toolkit, all twenty tools, is free for anyone who runs a game. Donations are the only thing that keeps this model sustainable, and even the price of a coffee genuinely helps.

Where your donation goes

Every contribution goes back into the project itself. In practice that means three things. First, infrastructure — Cloudflare Pages hosting is free, but the custom domain, SSL renewals and future scalability are not. Second, development time — hours spent building new tools, refining the DMG math, adding the 2024 rules compatibility you asked for, and fixing the edge cases you report. Third, staying independent — donations are what make it possible to say no to advertising and tracking, permanently.

Can't donate? Support it for free

Plenty of the most valuable support costs nothing at all. Share the tool with your DM group, your guild Discord, or subreddits like r/DMAcademy and r/dndnext — word of mouth is how this project grows. Star the repository if you use GitHub; stars push the project up search rankings and help other DMs find it. Report bugs and request tools — the roadmap is built almost entirely from table-level feedback, and a half-line message about what would save you time often becomes the next feature. Finally, link to the site from your own blog, campaign wiki or session notes; every honest link helps other tables find it.

Transparency promise: donating never unlocks features, removes limits, or grants early access — because there is nothing to unlock. There is no premium tier and there never will be. The code is MIT-licensed, so you can also self-host the entire toolkit yourself at zero cost; donating is purely a way to fund continued development for everyone.
