🧬 Templates & Transformations

Tool 9 of 20 · Part of the free 5e HP Forge toolkit · Runs offline, no tracking

Published adventures have used monster templates for decades: take an existing creature, graft on undead or draconic features, and get a 'new' monster for a fraction of the design work. The Templates tab does the same for your table, applying classic 5e transformations to any hit-dice chassis in one click.

Pick a preset and the tool rewrites the chassis deterministically, then shows the new expression and totals so nothing is hidden. Half-Dragon steps every die up one size and adds +2 HP per hit die — bigger frame, bigger breath weapon. Skeleton normalizes everything to d8s with no flat bonus, the classic reanimated chassis. Zombie adds +2 HP per hit die for its relentless durability. Vampire notes a regeneration of 20 HP per round, worth roughly +60 effective HP in a fight.

The Custom option opens four dials — die-size override, hit-dice change, Constitution change and flat-bonus change — a complete kit for inventing your own bloodlines, curses and blessings. One hill giant chassis plus four templates is four distinct encounters, each mechanically sound because the HP math underneath is done properly.

What you can do

  • Half-Dragon: dice step up one size, +2 HP per HD
  • Skeleton: every die becomes a d8, flat bonus zeroed
  • Zombie: +2 HP per hit die of relentless durability
  • Vampire: regeneration 20 noted at roughly +60 effective HP
  • Custom builder with four independent transformation dials
  • Before/after result box showing the new expression and totals

How the math works

Every template is a deterministic rewrite of the same four levers: die size, dice count, Constitution contribution and flat bonus. Because the transformation happens on the expression itself, the output stays a valid expression you can paste anywhere else in the toolkit — the numbers never go stale.

Each result prints a note describing exactly what changed — 'Die +1 step, +2 Con per HD' for Half-Dragon, for instance — then updates the main calculator with the new expression, so the transformation is applied everywhere in the toolkit at once.

Campaign trick: Skeleton and Zombie versions of one base creature make a great dungeon pair — same origin, different mechanics, zero extra prep.

Open this tool

Templates & Transformations lives inside the main calculator. Open the app and click the Templates tab — the ninth one in the tab bar. Everything computes client-side and works offline once the page has loaded.
