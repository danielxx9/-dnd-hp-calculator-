🛡️ Effective HP Calculator

Tool 3 of 20 · Part of the free 5e HP Forge toolkit · Runs offline, no tracking

Raw hit points lie. A creature immune to fire shrugs off half the party's damage; a boss that auto-succeeds three saves burns resources long before it drops. The Effective HP Calculator converts paper HP into real survivability, following the same defensive-adjustment logic the Dungeon Master's Guide uses when it prices traits into Challenge Rating.

Enter the base HP and the expected CR tier (CR 1–4, 5–10 or 11+), then tick the defensive traits: resistances and immunities across bludgeoning/piercing/slashing from nonmagical attacks plus ten damage types, from fire and cold to psychic and force. Each tier of defense multiplies the pool — one type ×1.25, two ×1.5, three or more ×2.0 — while any vulnerability cuts it in half. Legendary resistances, regeneration and temporary HP each add their own weight.

The Damage Per Round Estimation card finishes the job: give it the DPR your players actually deal and it reports how many rounds the creature realistically survives. Together the two cards answer the classic prep question — can this boss threaten my level 9 party? — with arithmetic instead of vibes.

What you can do

  • Resistance and immunity weighting across eleven damage types plus nonmagical B/P/S
  • CR-tier scaling, because traits cost far more at CR 3 than CR 15
  • Legendary resistances priced at +10, +20 or +30 per use by tier
  • Regeneration valued at three times its per-round rate
  • Vulnerabilities that halve the pool, not just a footnote
  • Survival-rounds estimate against any expected party DPR

How the math works

The engine multiplies base HP by the defense count (×1.25 / ×1.5 / ×2.0), halves it for vulnerabilities, then adds the legendary-resistance value per use, three times the regeneration rate and temporary HP flat. The result is the total damage the party must actually burn through before the monster drops.

The explanation box spells out every multiplier it applied — which types were counted, which tier they landed in — so the final total is auditable line by line rather than a single opaque number.

Table tip: immunity to nonmagical B/P/S is the strongest flag in the list — parties without magic weapons stall completely. Check the party's gear before giving it to a low-CR creature.

Open this tool
