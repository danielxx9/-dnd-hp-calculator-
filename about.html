About this tool

Free & open source under the MIT license

D&D 5e HP Calculator & Monster Builder — Ultimate Edition (short name: 5e HP Forge) is a free, browser-based toolkit for Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition Dungeon Masters and players. It bundles more than twenty interlocking tools into a single page: hit dice math, effective HP with resistances, CR reverse-engineering, a full PC builder, probability curves, statblock generation, encounter difficulty, an initiative tracker, Monte Carlo combat simulation and XP tracking.

Everything runs entirely in your browser. There is no account to create, no server to talk to, and nothing to install. Your saves, theme and settings live in your browser's local storage and never leave your device — see the Privacy Policy for the details.

Add-on modules

Three later releases layered extra modules on top of the core twenty, without touching the original tools:

  • v4 — DM extras: Loot Roller, Random Encounter generator, Lair & Legendary action builder, and a Kill Probability calculator with exact hit/crit/mitigation math.
  • v5 — power tools: Dice Sandbox (exploding dice, keep highest/lowest), Damage vs Target, Ability Score roller, Spell DC calculator, Conditions reference, CR Estimator, Rest & Recovery manager, d20 Odds with advantage/disadvantage, and an NPC Generator.
  • v6 — table & export: a searchable Bestiary of 80+ SRD creatures, Export & Print (Foundry VTT JSON, Roll20 JSON and paste text, printable statblock sheet), and a Settings panel with HP rounding mode and full backup/restore.

See the Changelog for the full history.

How it works

The whole application is three files — one HTML page, one stylesheet and one script — written in plain HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript (ES2020). There are no frameworks, no bundlers, no build step and no external requests. After your first visit, your browser's cache is enough to use the site offline.

State is persisted locally: an auto-save snapshot every 1.5 seconds while you type, up to 20 named save slots, the combat tracker state, XP history and your theme preference. The Settings tab can export everything as a JSON backup and restore it on any other device or browser.
