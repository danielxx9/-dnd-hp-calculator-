⚡ Combat Tracker

Tool 18 of 20 · Part of the free 5e HP Forge toolkit · Runs offline, no tracking

This is the tab that runs the whole fight. Add combatants — monsters or player characters — with a DEX modifier, max HP, AC and an optional duplicate count for goblin hordes. Initiative rolls automatically or by hand, the list sorts into strict order, and the round counter keeps the clock. Next Turn and Previous walk the order; Roll All, Re-sort, Undo Last and End Combat keep the machinery under your fingers.

Every combatant row carries a live HP bar with temporary-HP support, condition flags and its own death-save state. Damage and healing are logged chronologically with a full undo, so 'wait, the wizard took how much?' is a scroll, not an argument. Two import buttons pull in the monsters from your Encounter build and the saved party, which means the fight you designed is the fight you run — zero re-entry.

Everything auto-saves to the browser as you go, so a closed laptop, a crash or a cat on the power button costs you nothing. From first initiative roll to final death save, the fight lives in one place instead of a scatter of sticky notes.

What you can do

  • Initiative order with auto-roll, manual entry, re-sort and duplicates
  • Live HP bars with temporary HP per combatant
  • Conditions and death saves tracked on the same rows
  • Chronological damage and healing log with undo
  • One-click import from the Encounter Builder and party data
  • Round counter plus full auto-save to local storage

How the math works

Initiative is a d20 plus the DEX modifier, with the list re-sorted consistently after every change. HP bars render the fraction of maximum remaining, so drop-to-zero moments and over-healing are visible instantly, and the log's undo makes mistakes reversible rather than argumentative.

The summary tiles at the top of the tab show the fight's state at a glance — round number, active combatant, side sizes — so even on a shared screen the current turn is unmistakable.

Mook trick: use the Count field for identical creatures — six skeletons is one row and one initiative entry, not six, and the HP bar tracks the group.

Open this tool

Combat Tracker lives inside the main calculator. Open the app and click the Combat Tracker tab — the eighteenth one in the tab bar. Everything computes client-side and works offline once the page has loaded.
