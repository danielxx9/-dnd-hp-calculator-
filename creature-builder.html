🐉 Creature Builder

Tool 2 of 20 · Part of the free 5e HP Forge toolkit · Runs offline, no tracking

The Creature Builder turns the size-to-hit-die table into a one-click monster chassis. Pick the creature's size — Tiny, Small, Medium, Large, Huge or Gargantuan — and the tool selects the right hit die automatically: d4, d6, d8, d10, d12 or d20, exactly as the 5e monster-creation guidelines prescribe. No table-hopping, no second-guessing whether a Large brute rolls d8s or d10s.

Enter the number of hit dice, a Constitution modifier and any flat bonus, then press Build & Calculate. If you only know the Constitution score — 16 for a hardy brute, say — type that instead and press Set from Score; the tool converts the score to its modifier for you. The result grid reports average, minimum and maximum HP with a full breakdown of every term, so the chassis is documented the moment it exists.

This is the fastest path from 'I need something for tonight' to a creature with believable, size-appropriate hit points. Combine it with CR Reverse Engineering to pick the right dice count for a target difficulty, then hand the finished numbers to the Statblock Generator so the creature is properly written up.

What you can do

  • Automatic hit-die selection from size: Tiny d4 up to Gargantuan d20
  • Constitution score input with automatic modifier conversion
  • Flat-bonus field for template or homebrew padding
  • Average, minimum and maximum output with a full arithmetic breakdown
  • One-click build that feeds the Statblock Generator
  • Numbers consistent with how every published monster is constructed

How the math works

Hit points equal HD × average die + HD × Con modifier + flat bonus — the same construction published monsters use. A Medium creature with 8 HD, Constitution 16 and no flat bonus lands at 8 × 4.5 + 8 × 3 = 60 HP, and the breakdown spells out each contribution so you can defend the number at the table.

Design tip: match the die to the silhouette. Swarms and skulking things sit at the bottom of their size's range, brutes at the top — and a d20 hit die is nearly always Gargantuan, so players will notice if your Medium horror rolls one.

Open this tool
