🎲 Monster Design Tools

Tool 12 of 20 · Part of the free 5e HP Forge toolkit · Runs offline, no tracking

Four generator cards share this tab, all aimed at the same goal: populating a dungeon tonight. The Random Monster Generator rolls concept seeds filtered by target CR (1/8 to 20), creature type — all fifteen, from aberration to undead — and environment, from arctic coast to Underdark. It is inspiration with constraints, not a slot machine.

The Ability Score Generator produces a six-stat array appropriate to a chosen CR, shaped by one of five archetypes: Balanced, Brute, Skirmisher, Caster or Tank. The Skill Proficiency Calculator turns ability scores plus a CR-banded proficiency bonus into final modifiers for all eighteen skills — the tedious part of finishing any statblock.

The Lair & Legendary Action Impact card prices the boss-only bells and whistles: enter base HP and DPR, add lair-action damage and effect strength, count the legendary actions and their output, and the card reports the real combat weight those traits add. It is the difference between a boss who is legendary in name and one who is legendary on the initiative count.

What you can do

  • Random monster seeds filtered by CR, type and environment
  • Six-stat arrays across five combat archetypes
  • Skill proficiency math for all eighteen official skills
  • Proficiency bonus auto-banded from +2 at CR 0–4 to +9 at CR 29–30
  • Lair and legendary action output quantified in numbers
  • Every result ready to paste into the Statblock Generator

How the math works

The proficiency bonus follows the standard bands — +2 for CR 0–4, stepping up to +9 for CR 29–30 — and each skill's final modifier is its ability modifier plus that bonus. The lair-and-legendary card folds extra actions into an adjusted offensive estimate, so the hidden damage of bonus actions stops being invisible.

The random generator draws from SRD-flavored name pools and pairs each seed with sensible stat hooks, so a rolled 'swamp monstrosity at CR 4' arrives with enough substance to build on rather than a bare noun to invent around.

Workflow: roll three random seeds and keep the one that excites you, then rebuild it deliberately with the Ability Score Generator — randomness for inspiration, math for execution.
