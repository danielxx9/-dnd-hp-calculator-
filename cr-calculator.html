🎯 Challenge Rating Calculator

Tool 14 of 20 · Part of the free 5e HP Forge toolkit · Runs offline, no tracking

The CR Calculator is the Dungeon Master's Guide page-274 process turned into a form. Feed it a creature's Armor Class and hit points, its attack bonus and damage per round, and an optional save DC, plus a rough count of its resistances and immunities, and it computes both halves of the official rating: defensive CR from survivability, offensive CR from damage output, then the final Challenge Rating.

The defensive side rewards tanks — high AC and a big HP pool push it up, with the resistance count (none, one or two, three or more, many immunities) weighed the way the DMG's defensive-adjustment step does. The offensive side looks at how hard the creature hits and how accurately. Four special-trait checkboxes apply the known adjustments: Magic Resistance, a fly speed, legendary actions and lair actions.

The result panel shows the defensive, offensive and final CRs side by side, so you can see whether your creation is a glass cannon or a durable slog — and rebalance it before the players find out the hard way.

What you can do

  • Full DMG p.274 pipeline: defensive CR, offensive CR, final average
  • Resistance and immunity tiers priced into the defensive half
  • Save DC included on the offensive side for casting creatures
  • Magic Resistance, fly speed, legendary and lair action adjustments
  • Glass-cannon diagnosis from the two CR halves diverging
  • The definitive check before homebrew meets the party

How the math works

The final rating is the average of the defensive and offensive halves. A creature that hits like a truck and folds like cardboard lands in between, not at its scariest number — which is exactly why the two halves are shown separately. Traits such as legendary actions add fixed steps, matching the DMG's guidance for boss creatures.

The defensive half of the rating uses the HP, AC and resistance inputs, while the offensive half uses DPR, attack bonus and save DC — keep the halves in view together and the creature's combat personality is visible at a glance.

Balance check: if the defensive and offensive CRs differ by more than a couple of steps, rebalance before play — raise the HP of the glass cannon or the DPR of the wall.
