📈 XP & Loot Tracker

Tool 20 of 20 · Part of the free 5e HP Forge toolkit · Runs offline, no tracking

Bookkeeping is the least heroic part of DMing, and this tab does all of it. Enter the party's current level and XP, add each session's gained XP with one click, and the tracker logs the history, shows progress toward the next level, and predicts when the level-up lands. The history is exportable, so the campaign's numeric memory survives browser cleanup and travels between devices with the rest of your backups.

The Loot & XP Split Calculator handles the end-of-session arithmetic before it becomes an argument. Enter total gold, total XP, party size and how many magic items dropped, and the tool divides the shares evenly and accounts for the items. Four adventurers clearing a hoard get exact numbers instead of approximations — and the haggling stays in character.

Whether your campaign runs strict XP, milestones or something homebrewed, organized numbers pay off: they settle 'are we nearly there?', let you pace the next session against real progress, and reveal when the party has been stuck at level 7 for six sessions and quietly needs a rich dungeon.

What you can do

  • Campaign XP log with per-session entries and full history
  • Level-up prediction against the official XP thresholds
  • Exportable history for backups and device moves
  • Loot split by GP, XP, party size and magic-item count
  • One-click session updates, plus a reset for new arcs
  • Pairs with the Day Budget tab for pacing between sessions

How the math works

Levels advance through the published XP thresholds, and the tracker reports the distance to the next gate both in XP and in sessions at your current pace — so 'two more sessions' replaces the vague feeling. The loot split divides GP and XP evenly by party size and lists the magic items separately.

Because the history is plain data, it pairs naturally with the Settings tab's backup export — restore the backup on a new machine and the campaign's numbers come with it.

Log it fresh: record XP right after the session while the tally is known. Reconstructed XP is how parties drift two levels away from your plans.

Open this tool

XP & Loot Tracker lives inside the main calculator. Open the app and click the XP Tracker tab — the twentieth one in the tab bar. Everything computes client-side and works offline once the page has loaded.
