How the tools connect

The suite is designed as a pipeline, not twenty isolated widgets. Start with the Hit Dice Calculator or the Creature Builder for a chassis, check it with CR Reverse Engineering, price its defenses with Effective HP, verify the whole stat block with the CR Calculator, and write it up in the Statblock Generator. Then plug the creature into the Encounter Builder, pace the day with the Adventuring Day Budget, simulate the matchup in the Monte Carlo Combat Simulator, and run the fight live in the Combat Tracker — logging the rewards in the XP & Loot Tracker afterwards.

Everything runs locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is tracked, and every feature works offline once the page has loaded. See the Privacy Policy for the details and the Changelog for what each release added.
