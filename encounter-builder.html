🏰 Encounter Builder

Tool 10 of 20 · Part of the free 5e HP Forge toolkit · Runs offline, no tracking

The Encounter Builder is the Dungeon Master's Guide encounter math, automated. Build the party — add PCs by level, or press Quick Party for the standard four level-3 characters — then list the monsters by CR and count. One click runs the full official pipeline: per-monster XP, the group multiplier, the adjusted total, and a comparison against the party's easy, medium, hard and deadly thresholds.

The verdict is drawn on a difficulty spectrum bar, so you can see at a glance whether you built a warm-up, a proper fight or a funeral. Below it, the Action Economy panel counts turns per side — the most underrated difficulty factor in 5e. Four skeletons against four PCs reads 'easy' on paper and routinely plays deadly, because twenty-one turns a round grind the party down between their own turns.

The Multi-Creature HP Tracker card completes the tab: it pulls every monster from your encounter into a live HP list, so the numbers you designed on Tuesday are the numbers you actually track when the dice start flying on Friday.

What you can do

  • Party builder with per-PC levels and a one-click standard party
  • Monster list by CR and count, any mix
  • Official XP thresholds: easy, medium, hard, deadly
  • Group multiplier from ×1 for a lone monster to ×4 for fifteen-plus
  • Visual difficulty bar plus a written verdict
  • Action economy summary and a synced multi-creature HP tracker

How the math works

Adjusted XP equals raw XP times the group multiplier — 1.5 for a pair, 2 for three to six, 2.5 for seven to ten, and up to 4 for fifteen or more — then the total is compared against each party threshold to name the difficulty. The tool shows the arithmetic, so you can defend the rating.

Tracker rows update as you take damage during the fight, and a refresh button re-pulls from the encounter design if you change the monster list mid-session, so the design and the live tracking never drift apart.

Read the economy: a 'deadly' single boss is often fairer than a 'hard' mob of twelve mooks. Check the turns-per-side line before committing to the fight.

Open this tool

Encounter Builder lives inside the main calculator. Open the app and click the Encounter tab — the tenth one in the tab bar. Everything computes client-side and works offline once the page has loaded.
